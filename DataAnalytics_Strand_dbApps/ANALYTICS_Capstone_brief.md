# dbApps Analytics Capstone — Brief (reframes the dbApps capstone)

**Course**: Database Applications (Junior) — Medina County Career Center

Instead of a pure-SQL capstone, students run a **complete analysis** end-to-end and communicate one insight.

## The task (~1 week)
Pick a dataset (the class `pizza_orders.csv`, or a richer one — NBA, IMDB, or a civic dataset your teacher
provides). Then:

1. **Ask** — write one clear, answerable question ("Which region should we open a new store in?").
2. **Get** — pull the data with **SQL** (or load the CSV).
3. **Clean** — handle missing/duplicate/outlier values; note what you did.
4. **Analyze** — compute the descriptive stats and group-bys that answer your question (Excel **or** Python).
5. **Visualize** — build the right chart(s) for your question (labeled).
6. **Communicate** — assemble a short **Tableau dashboard** (or a slide) with your answer and evidence.

## Deliverables
- The SQL query (or load step) used.
- A short analysis (Excel workbook or Jupyter notebook) with stats + at least two charts.
- A Tableau dashboard (or 2–3 slides) stating **one insight** with the chart that supports it.
- A 3–5 sentence written conclusion.

## Rubric (100 pts)
| Section | Pts | Looking for |
|---|---|---|
| Clear question | 10 | answerable, specific |
| Get + Clean | 20 | correct query/load; documented cleaning |
| Analyze | 25 | right stats/group-bys; correct numbers |
| Visualize | 25 | right chart for the question; labeled |
| Communicate | 20 | one clear insight + supporting evidence; dashboard/slide |

**Ties the strand together:** it exercises the SQL/pandas from dbApps *and* the Excel→Python→Tableau
analytics progression — and mirrors what the ITS Data Analytics cert rewards.
