# Data Analytics Drill: Choose the Chart & Read the Visualization

**Course**: Database Applications / Data Analytics (CTE) | **Instructor**: Ryan McMaster — Medina County Career Center  
**Rehearses the exam's visualization items — pick the right chart for the question, and judge a chart's design.**

## Part A — Pick the Best Chart

Choose from: **bar chart · histogram · line chart · scatter plot · pie chart · heatmap**

1. Show the **distribution** of pizza order sizes (how many orders fall in each size range) → `____`
2. Show a **trend over time** (monthly sales across a year) → `____`
3. Compare a **total across categories** (sales per region) → `____`
4. Show the **relationship between two numeric variables** (ad spend vs. revenue) → `____`
5. Show each part's **share of a whole** (budget split by department) → `____`

## Part B — Visualization Best Practices (mark GOOD or BAD practice)

6. Add a **caption** explaining how to interpret the chart → `Good / Bad`
7. Use a **distinct color** to highlight the data point that matters → `Good / Bad`
8. Start a bar chart's y-axis at a number other than zero to exaggerate differences → `Good / Bad`
9. Use 12 nearly-identical shades of blue for 12 categories → `Good / Bad`
10. Label axes and include units → `Good / Bad`

## Part C — Draw a Conclusion

11. A scatter plot of study-hours vs. test-score shows points rising left-to-right in a tight band. What can you conclude? → `____________`
12. A histogram of incomes has a long right tail (a few very high values). The **mean** will be ____ the **median**: `higher / lower / equal`.
